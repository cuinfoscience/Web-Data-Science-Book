## What the live checks found (2026-11-07, from a cloud machine)

**Datalab** (`POST https://www.datalab.to/api/v1/convert` with `file_url`, then poll `request_check_url`):
- `balanced` mode converted all seven reports in 12–15 seconds each: 106 pages for 31.8 cents (0.3 cents a page). The free allowance covers about 3,000 pages a month.
- `lite` mode cost 0.56 cents for the 15-page 2024 report, but its tables are rougher.
- It read Exhibit 3, the **picture of a table** the city says to phone for a "screen readable version", as a Markdown table.
- It wrote **descriptions of charts that are wrong**. Chart 8's description calls \$1,262,688 the largest slice and then names \$1,341,007. For the sugar-sweetened beverage bar chart, it wrote a whole table of estimates ("~\$315,000") that appear nowhere in the PDF. pdfplumber's text layer has the chart's axis only.

**OpenRouter, structured output** (`response_format` with `json_schema`, `strict`):
- Of 12 `:free` models, 5 accept `structured_outputs`. Most answered **429, rate-limited upstream**, at some point. A class sharing the free pool will meet this, so the lab sends a `models` fallback list, and the chapter explains it.
- `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl:free` copied the 2024 totals exactly. It gave the year as 2023 on one run and 2024 on the next, at temperature 0.
- `dots-studio/dots-3-note-preview:free` swapped the two years and took the subtotal row: every figure wrong, in valid JSON.
- **Across the seven reports, 5 of 7 totals matched chapter 9.** In 2018 and 2019 the summary table runs onto the next page. The page sent held only the subtotal, and the model returned it as the total. Months and years were also wrong: "January" from "received through January 2019", and 2025.

**OpenRouter, embeddings** (`POST /api/v1/embeddings`):
- `nvidia/nemotron-3-embed-1b:free` (2,048 dimensions) embeds batches.
- The newer free 3.5 models fail on batches (502), and the 8b only takes one input at a time.
- Inputs are capped at 4,096 tokens, so pages are split into chunks of about 2,500 characters: 243 chunks.
- Retrieval finds topics (marijuana tax, the 2020 decline) but not figures in tables. Filtering to one report first does better.

**MCP:** DeepWiki's public server, `https://mcp.deepwiki.com/mcp`, answers `initialize` and `tools/list` with no login. It answers as server-sent events (`data: {...}`). Its tools describe their inputs with JSON Schema, the same language as Wednesday's `response_format`. The current specification is 2026-07-28; the server accepted 2025-06-18.

**Skills:** `anthropics/skills`' [`pdf/SKILL.md`](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/pdf/SKILL.md) is plain Markdown, with front matter (`name`, `description`) and instructions that use pypdf and pdfplumber, chapter 9's tools.
