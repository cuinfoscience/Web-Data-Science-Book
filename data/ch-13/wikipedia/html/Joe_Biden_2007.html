Joseph Robinette "Joe" Biden, Jr. (born 20 November 1942) is an American lawyer and politician from Wilmington, Delaware. He is a member of the Democratic Party and the incumbent senior U.S. Senator from Delaware. Biden is currently serving his sixth term and is Delaware's longest-serving Senator. He has served as Chairman of the U.S. Senate Committee on the Judiciary and the U.S. Senate Committee on Foreign Relations, is the probable Chairman of the U.S. Senate Committee on Foreign Relations in the 110th Congress. He has declared his intent to be a candidate in the 2008 presidential election numerous times, though he has yet to file with the Federal Election Commission.
