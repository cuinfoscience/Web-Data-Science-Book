Donald John Trump was born on June 14, 1946 (in Queens, New York) to Mary MacLeod Trump, a native of Scotland, and the soon-to-be millionaire Fred Trump. Trump is an American business executive, entrepreneur and author. He is the CEO of Trump Organization, an American-based real estate developer in the real estate market and the founder of Trump Entertainment, which operates gambling casinos. He enjoyed a great deal of publicity following the success of his reality television show, The Apprentice (in which he serves as both executive producer and host for the show).
