Joseph Robinette Biden Jr. (born November 20, 1942) is an American politician. A Democrat and U.S. Senator, Biden has represented Delaware in Congress since 1972. Biden failed in his bid to win the Democratic nomination for President in 1988.

Biden is the ranking member of the Senate Foreign Relations Committee and the Judiciary Subcommittee on Crime. His experience in foreign policy and arms control issues has won him considerable respect.
