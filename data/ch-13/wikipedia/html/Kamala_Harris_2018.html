Kamala Devi Harris (/ˈkɑːmələ/, KAH-mə-lə;[1] born October 20, 1964) is an American politician and lawyer. A Democrat, she currently serves as the junior United States Senator from California. She previously was the Attorney General of California.

Harris graduated from Howard University and UC Hastings. After working in the San Francisco District Attorney's office and City Attorney's office Harris was elected District Attorney of San Francisco. Harris was elected California's Attorney General in 2010 and re-elected in 2014.[2][3]

On November 8, 2016, she defeated Loretta Sanchez in the 2016 Senate election to succeed outgoing Senator Barbara Boxer, becoming California's third female U. S. Senator and the first United States Senator of Jamaican and Indian descent.[4][5] As a senator, she has generally opposed President Donald Trump's policies.[6]
