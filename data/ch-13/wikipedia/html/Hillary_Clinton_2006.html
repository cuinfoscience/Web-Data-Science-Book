Hillary Rodham Clinton (born Hillary Diane Rodham on October 26, 1947) is the junior United States Senator from New York, serving her freshman term since January 3, 2001. As the wife of President Bill Clinton, she served as the White House Hostess, styled the First Lady of the United States, from 1993 to 2001. Before that she was a prominent lawyer and First Lady of Arkansas. She is a member of the Democratic Party.
